Highlights

- Model couples char removal, glass sintering, crystallisation and copper co-firing
- Steam held at a fraction of the Cu/Cu2O limit reproduces the IBM burnout window
- Fine copper sinters during burnout, far ahead of the glass: 15.6 % mismatch
- Releasing copper by char removal synchronises both, but the optimum is fragile
- Char gasification kinetics and glass viscosity are the first things to measure
