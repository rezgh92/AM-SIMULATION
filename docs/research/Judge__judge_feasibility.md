  - FROM ARCH 2 — the flux-limited regime proof and the closed-form design law, as the spine of both the week-0 sizing tool and the paper. t_min = dH_c*rho_b*(V/A)/(h*dT_crit) is parameter-free, dimensionally exact, and I reproduced it: 47.7 h at 5 mm half-thickness with h=20 W/m2K and dT=20 K, 95.5 h at 10 mm, and 9.5 h at 5 mm if h is raised to 100. Grafting the law costs one afternoon and it is simultaneously the sizing tool, the acceptance test for the exotherm module, and the paper's central claim.
  - FROM ARCH 2 — the isokinetic reparameterisation, k(T) = exp(kappa - (E/R)(1/T - 1/T_iso)) with kappa = ln k(T_iso) and T_iso ~ 624 K. In the raw (lnA, E) parameterisation the Jacobian columns over a 200-500 C TGA window are correlated at -0.999857, which makes any sampler mix badly and any optimiser report false precision. This is a one-line change to every Arrhenius term in Arch 3's L1 (binder pseudo-components, Cu oxidation, Cu2O reduction incubation, gasification, grain growth) and it costs nothing. Take it immediately.
  - FROM ARCH 2 — the triage table with an IDENTIFIED / MARGINALISED / IRRELEVANT verdict for every parameter, plus the discipline of running global sensitivity analysis BEFORE committing furnace hours. Arch 3's provenance gate (CI fails if a GUESS-tagged parameter appears in the top-5 Sobol indices of an active constraint) is the same idea, better implemented and cheaper; the graft is Arch 2's explicit three-bucket verdict and the rule that an experiment sharpening an IRRELEVANT parameter must visibly score zero.
  - FROM ARCH 1 — the exhaust-gas closed loop. A zirconia oxygen sensor plus a dew-point meter plus NDIR CO2 (or a small RGA) on the retort outlet is the cheapest instrument in the whole project that converts binder ignorance into feedback. It measures, in real time and without knowing anything about the binder, the two quantities the ordering constraints are written on: how much carbon is actually leaving, and the realised oxygen potential. It is far easier to retrofit than slaving furnace temperature to a shrinkage signal, it turns every Arch 3 furnace run into a calibration dataset instead of a pass/fail, and it directly fixes Arch 3's weakest point, which is that Cycle Card v0 runs blind. Buy it in week 1 alongside the bubbler and heated line.
  - FROM ARCH 1 — the NEML2 sintering-stress check as a day-one unit test, before any 3-D work. The driving force vanishes precisely in the 92-99% window that separates 'another 90%-dense copper part' from a result. Arch 3 has this as ADR-0007, which is the right place, but it should be promoted from a design decision to a committed regression test with all three forms selectable and a documented default. Cost: one day. Value: the difference between densification stalling and not.
