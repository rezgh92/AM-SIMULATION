- **name**: CUPOLA — Copper Unknown-binder Pragmatic Optimal-control Loop Architecture
- **thesis**: The bet: for this material the binding constraints are 0-D and chemical, not 3-D and mechanical, so a small fast model plus a fast measurement campaign will produce a defensible cycle in three weeks, and the elite simulation should be grown around that loop rather than in front of it. I verified this numerically before committing: (a) the Darcy over-pressure for a 16 um Cu powder at 2% open porosity, 5 mm half-thickness, 10%-binder-per-hour is 2.4 bar against a measured green strength of 10-23 MPa (100-230 bar) — a 40-100x margin, so the classic ceramic pressure/cracking constraint is INACTIVE and the expensive gas-transport PDE branch can be demoted to a diagnostic; (b) the Cu/Cu2O reducing margin is enormous — at 1000 C with 1000 ppm H2 you would need a dew point above +50 C to approach the boundary with 100x safety, so atmosphere control is not about protecting the copper, it is about maximising carbon gasification rate against the specific-surface-area clock; (c) the constraint that actually binds during debinding is the exotherm, and it is brutal and purely geometric: keeping part-to-furnace excess below 20 K at h = 20 W/m2K requires spreading 1.25e10 J/m3 of binder combustion over 8.7 h at 1 mm half-thickness, 43 h at 5 mm and 87 h at 10 mm — which derives Fraunhofer IFAM's otherwise mysterious 63 h / 250 C hold from first principles and tells you the leading control variable in the pyrolysis phase is pO2, not dT/dt. The architecture therefore puts a CasADi-differentiable 0-D screener and a measured feedstock "State Card" at the centre, treats the unknown binder as a measured four-phase inventory rather than a chemistry, forces the single biggest scientific fork (oxidise-the-copper-then-reduce vs. never-oxidise-and-gasify-the-char-with-steam) into one two-arm furnace campaign in week 3, and escalates to 1-D reactive transport and 3-D MOOSE/NEML2 poroviscoplasticity only where a written, numeric escalation rule says the data demands it.
- **layers**:
  -
    - **layer**: L0 — Feedstock State Card (measurement-as-code)
    - **purpose**: Replace every proprietary unknown with a measured number carrying a provenance tag, before any simulation is trusted. This is the foundation of the whole system and the reason the project can move fast: the dominant uncertainty is a one-day bench measurement, not a modelling output.
    - **physics_or_math**: Pure mass/volume balance, but with one non-obvious closure that is the keystone of the project — LOI-THEN-REDUCE. m0 (cured, solvent-washed, dried green coupon, 2-5 g) -> muffle 600 C / air / 4 h -> m1 (= Cu_xO_y + inorganic ash) -> tube furnace 4%H2/Ar / 400 C / 2 h (Ott: native-oxide reduction complete at 350 C in <30 min; allow 2 h for bulk oxide) -> m2 (= Cu + ash). Then m_binder = m0 - m2 exactly, independent of how much oxygen the copper picked up in step 2. Balance resolution +/-0.1 mg on 2 g = 0.005 wt%, against an expected binder load of 7.3-13.6 wt% (computed: 45 vol% -> 12.5-13.6 wt%, 50 -> 10.5-11.4, 55 -> 8.8-9.5, 60 -> 7.3-7.9 for rho_b = 1.05-1.15 g/cm3). phi_green = (m2/8960)/V_geom; eps_open,green = 1 - rho_bulk(Archimedes, wax-sealed)/rho_true(He pycnometry).   -
    - **layer**: L1 — 0-D Screener (the workhorse; CasADi-differentiable)
    - **purpose**: The model the optimiser calls 1e5 times and the model that produces the cycle the furnace actually runs. Solves in <1 s. Everything else in the stack exists to correct or extend this.
    - **physics_or_math**: State y = [T_part, S_solv, alpha_1..alpha_3, X_ox, X_red, C_char, rho_rel, G, eps_open, n_O, n_C]. Energy (lumped, justified by Bi = hL/k_eff = 2e-4 to 0.67 across every realistic case): rho_b c_p dT/dt = sum_i dH_i r_i + dH_ox r_ox - (hA/V)(T - T_f) - (eps_r sigma A/V)(T^4 - T_f^4). Binder: dalpha_i/dt = A_i exp(-E_i/RT) c_i alpha_i^m_i (1-alpha_i)^n_i [1 + k_i (pO2/p0)^0.5], truncated Sestak-Berggren seeded at the random-scission values m = 0.39-0.40, n = 1.02-1.12 (NOT the inverted literal table reading — unit-tested against the numerically integrated Simha-Wall solution for L = 2..8). Solvent: Clausius-Clapeyron VLE + Darcy/Knudsen removal, constant-rate then falling-rate period, NOT an Arrhenius alpha. Cu oxidation: parabolic, dX_ox/dt = k_p0 exp(-Q_ox/RT) pO2^(1/n) / (2 X_ox), Q_ox = 111 kJ/mol above 873 K and 40 kJ/mol below 773 K (Mater. Trans. 43 (2002) 2173), anchored to the three Roumanie TGA points +8.0 / +14.4 / +16.8 wt% at 400/600/800 C. Cu2O reduction: JMAK with an explicit nucleation incubation tau_inc = tau0 exp(E_inc/RT), E_red = 114.6 kJ/mol (Cu2O+H2), 60.7 kJ/mol (CuO+H2) — a plain first-order Arrhenius predicts reduction far too early and hides the blistering risk. Char gasification: random-pore model dC/dt = -k_g S(C) pH2O/(1 + K_H2 pH2), S(C) = C(1 - psi ln C)^0.5, Ea prior 176-222 kJ/mol, and this rate law is the single largest unmeasured quantity in the system (see E6). Densification: SOVS in Sandia's verified free-sintering form xi_dot = -3 sigma_s0 xi/(4 eta0), with eta0(T) recovered POINTWISE by Balaguer inversion eta0 = -3 sigma_s(rho)/(18 eps_dot_in psi(rho) lambda(rho)), lambda = (a5/(rho_c - rho))^b5, Olevsky's seven closure constants (a1=1, b1=2, a2=2/3, b2=3, c2=1, a3=1, b3=2) held fixed at their theoretical values. Grain growth G^3 - G0^3 = k_G t exp(-Q_G/RT). Diagnostics computed but not integrated: Biot; Frank-Kamenetskii delta = Q rho A E L^2/(k_eff R T^2) exp(-E/RT) vs 0.88 (slab); Darcy dP = mu n_dot R T L^2/(2 K M P) with Kozeny-Carman K = eps^3 d^2/(180(1-eps)^2); reducing margin m(T) = (pH2/pH2O)_act/(pH2/pH2O)_eq(T).
    - **implementation**: src/amculcm/models/l1_zero_d.py. Written twice against one shared parameter object: a numpy/scipy solve_ivp (BDF, analytic Jacobian) reference for debugging and plotting, and a CasADi SX expression graph for the optimiser, with a CI test that the two agree to 1e-8 on a fixed cycle. Gas thermodynamics from Cantera (custom Cu-C-H-O phase) or the hand-rolled Ellingham module; Arden Buck for dew point <-> pH2O.
    - **inputs**: State Card (L0); kinetic triplets from L5; atmosphere setpoint trajectory; h from L3; geometry as a single half-thickness L and A/V.
    - **outputs**: Full state trajectory; active-constraint timeline; terminal C, O, rho, shrinkage; predicted TGA/DTG/dilatometry traces for direct overlay on measurements.
    - **effort**: ~2000 lines, 3 weeks for one competent person. This is the highest-value single deliverable in the project.
  -
    - **layer**: L2 — 1-D Reactive-Transport Slab (thickness transfer)
    - **purpose**: Explains and predicts the ONE thing L1 structurally cannot: why a 3 mm gear comes out at 0.022 wt% O and a 12.6 x 3 mm disc at 0.062 wt% O under an identical cycle (Roumanie Table 5). That is a ready-made, published thickness-transfer validation dataset.
    - **physics_or_math**: Method of lines, 200 cells, half-thickness domain with symmetry. Continuity/Darcy: d(eps rho_g)/dt + d/dx[-(K/mu) rho_g dP/dx] = sum ṁ_gen. Species k in {O2, H2, H2O, CO, CO2, CH4, HC}: d(eps c_k)/dt + d/dx[v c_k - eps D_k_eff dc_k/dx] = R_k, with D_k_eff = (eps/tau)(1/D_mol + 1/D_Kn)^-1, tau = eps^-0.5 (Bruggeman), D_Kn = (2r_h/3) sqrt(8RT/pi M). Copper catalyses the water-gas shift, so CO + H2O <-> CO2 + H2 must be included as a fast surface reaction — otherwise the model lets internal pCO run to absurd values and misses the in-situ H2 source that feeds blistering. Energy: (rho c)_eff dT/dt = d/dx(k_eff dT/dx) - rho_g c_pg v dT/dx + sum dH_i r_i, with k_eff(rho, phi_binder, T) spanning three orders of magnitude — Maxwell-Eucken with a polymer continuous phase in the green state (computed 0.80-1.10 W/mK at 50-60 vol% Cu, NOT copper-like), crossing to a Bruggeman/percolation brown-body branch, to ~390 W/mK dense. Anisotropic K_z != K_xy carried as a ratio parameter.
    - **implementation**: src/amculcm/models/l2_slab_1d.py — plain numpy + scipy.integrate.solve_ivp with a banded analytic Jacobian, deliberately not FiPy/FEniCS (faster to debug, easier to ship). Shares the exact same kinetics/thermochem modules as L1 by import, never by copy.
    - **inputs**: Same as L1 plus the surface gas composition from L3 and the part half-thickness ladder from E2.
    - **outputs**: T(x,t), P(x,t), carbon and oxygen profiles through thickness, the radial carbon gradient that oxygen starvation produces, the thickness at which the cycle stops being safe.
    - **effort**: ~900 lines, 2 weeks. Built in Phase 2, not Phase 0.
  -
    - **layer**: L3 — Retort / Furnace CSTR (the gas-supply balance everyone omits)
    - **purpose**: Closes the oxygen mass balance at furnace scale and calibrates h, which is the most sensitive unmeasured parameter in the entire debinding model (every self-heating number scales as 1/h). Every published debinding model in the brief's literature treats atmosphere as a boundary-condition label; this layer is what makes atmosphere a genuine control.
    - **physics_or_math**: N tanks-in-series CSTR (N = 3-5, fitted from a tracer step test): dn_k/dt = Q_in c_k,in - Q_out c_k + sum_parts A_p J_k(surface). Stoichiometric oxygen demand computed from the State Card: at 50 vol% Cu the binder is ~550 kg/m3 and an acrylate needs ~1.67 kg O2/kg binder, i.e. ~920 kg O2 per m3 of green body; a 2 slpm air feed delivers 0.010 g O2/s, so a full CeraFab build platform (76 x 43 x 170 mm envelope) is oxygen-starved by orders of magnitude and the interior of the retort pyrolyses while the exterior combusts. Heat transfer: h fitted from an embedded-thermocouple run (E7); radiative term with emissivity as a function of oxidation state (clean Cu eps ~ 0.05 gives a 20 K furnace-to-part lag at 5 K/min, oxidised eps ~ 0.30 gives 3 K). Actuator model maps what the operator can actually set — (gas blend, bubbler temperature, mass-flow setpoints, total pressure) — onto (pO2, pH2, pH2O) at the part surface. This is what makes an optimised trajectory executable rather than notional.
    - **implementation**: src/amculcm/models/l3_retort.py; Cantera for gas-phase equilibrium and transport; src/amculcm/thermochem/gas.py for Arden Buck and the H2/H2O/CO/CO2 equilibrium pO2.
    - **inputs**: Furnace geometry and free volume; MFC ranges; bubbler curve; tracer step test; embedded-TC log.
    - **outputs**: h (W/m2K) with uncertainty; surface gas composition trajectory; the loading limit above which a cycle validated on coupons will fail on a full platform.
    - **effort**: ~500 lines, 1 week, plus one tracer test and one instrumented run.
  -
    - **layer**: L4 — 3-D Thermo-Chemo-Mechanical FE (distortion only)
    - **purpose**: The only thing the cheap layers genuinely cannot do: anisotropic shrinkage, gravity sag, setter drag, interlayer delamination and geometry-specific distortion compensation. Deliberately the LAST thing built.
    - **physics_or_math**: Finite-strain multiplicative split F = F_e F_th F_chem F_in. F_chem carries the oxidation eigenstrain, which no ceramic debinding model contains and which dominates the dimensional budget: Pilling-Bedworth 1.68 for Cu2O means Roumanie's measured +8 wt% (64% conversion) is a +43 vol% / +12.7% linear expansion of the metal phase, and Resch measured +3.0% diametral / +7.8% build-axis on real DLP discs. F_in from SOVS/GTN poroviscoplasticity: sigma_ij = 2 eta0 [phi(rho) eps_dot'_ij + psi(rho) tr(eps_dot) delta_ij] + P_L delta_ij, phi = rho^2, psi = (2/3) rho^3/(1-rho), with ANISOTROPIC sintering stress (P_lR, P_lZ) to reproduce the measured 15.5-16.8% in-plane vs 17.9-21.7% build-axis shrinkage. Gravity body force; Coulomb friction on the setter with mu = 0.87-1.31 measured at 1100 C by Blunk & Seibel — 3-4x Simufact's default of 0.3, and the difference is the distortion answer. Transversely isotropic green elasticity plus an explicit interlayer cohesive zone, so delamination and translayer cracking are reported as SEPARATE failure modes, which is how every experimental dataset labels them.
    - **implementation**: Upstream idaholab/moose (which vendors applied-material-modeling/neml2 as framework/contrib/neml2 and ships scripts/update_and_rebuild_neml2.sh) — NOT the PUMA fork, which has zero CI, pins submodules by moving branch, and contains no sintering code at all (grep -ril sinter over the whole repo matches only README.md) and whose mechanics is purely thermoelastic. Start from NEML2's tests/regression/solid_mechanics/viscoplasticity/free_sintering/model.i, which already composes ThermalEigenstrain + OlevskySinteringStress + GTNYieldFunction (the sintering-stress offset is already in the cosh term) + PerzynaPlasticFlowRate + VoceIsotropicHardening + GursonCavitation under backward-Euler ImplicitUpdate. Swap in copper: Frost & Ashby power-law n = 4.8 (not NEML2's placeholder n = 2), A = 7.4e5, mu(T) = 42.1 GPa x (1 + (T-300)/1356 x -0.54), D0v = 2e-5 m2/s with Qv = 197 kJ/mol, delta D0b = 5e-15 m3/s with Qb = 104 kJ/mol. Lift PUMA's six coupled kernels (PumaCoupledTimeDerivative/Diffusion/DarcyFlow, CoupledL2Projection, CoupledMaterialSource, CoupledDirichletBC — 1514 lines total) as PATTERNS into our own MOOSE app. FIRST TASK before any calibration: settle on paper whether NEML2's sigma_s = 3 gamma phi^2/r (void fraction, vanishes at full density) or the classical 3 gamma rho^2/r (peaks at full density) is right — they differ qualitatively in exactly the late-stage regime that decides whether copper hits target density. If NEML2's form is wrong for us, the corrected model is ~60 lines of PyTorch.
    - **inputs**: Frozen 0-D densification kernel from L5; phi(rho) from the deviatoric probe E5; friction from literature or E5b; part CAD.
    - **outputs**: Shrinkage per axis, distortion field, residual stress, delamination/translayer crack risk maps, and a geometry compensation (inverse-scaled STL).
    - **effort**: 8-12 weeks including the verification suite. Gated on Phase 3.
  -
    - **layer**: L5 — Calibration, Identifiability and UQ
    - **purpose**: Keeps the project honest and stops it wasting months fitting parameters the data cannot resolve. The brief contains two specific identifiability traps and this layer is built around them.
    - **physics_or_math**: Trap 1, EXACT structural degeneracy: in free sintering only the RATIO sigma_s0/eta0 appears (Sandia Eq. 31), so free dilatometry at any number of heating rates cannot separate them — log-scaled Fisher information eigenvalues 7.7e-10 / 2.1e-3 / 1.16e3, condition number 1.5e12. Fix: a DEVIATORIC probe with DUAL-AXIS strain. Note the sharpened version — the TRACE of the SOVS rate is phi-free even under an applied uniaxial load (Sandia Eq. 32 contains no phi), so a sinter-forge run measuring only density or only axial length is exactly as blind as free dilatometry. You must measure axial AND radial strain, or use a gravity-sag overhang bar where the body force is independent of sigma_s0. Trap 2, the lnA-E compensation ridge in the kinetics. Fix: ICTAC-2020-compliant isoconversional stack in strict order — Friedman first (works on ramps, isotherms and CRTA), Vyazovkin advanced/flexible integral with piecewise integration as the reference when Ea(alpha) varies, KAS/OFW AUTOMATICALLY DISABLED when max-min Ea over alpha in [0.1,0.9] exceeds 15% of the mean, Kissinger as initial guess only. Model selection over N = 2..4 parallel SB reactions by BIC. Then Bayesian calibration (NUTS in PyMC, or emcee for the stiff cases) with provenance-aware priors. Kennedy-O'Hagan discrepancy delta(x) is FORBIDDEN by a CI test until all known missing physics is in the state vector — reducing an 8 wt% oxygen pickup generates 22,400 mol H2O per m3 of green body against ~6,400 mol/m3 from the entire binder, and no discrepancy term can absorb a missing source term 3.5x larger than the one you kept. Global sensitivity by Morris screening then Sobol via SALib, but deliberately SMALL: seven of SOVS's eleven constants are Olevsky's theoretical values and are held fixed, so the real identification problem is 2-4 parameters, not 11.
    - **implementation**: src/amculcm/calibration/{identifiability.py, bayes.py, discrepancy.py}; pkynetics or a hand-rolled isoconversional module (validated against ICTAC test data); SALib; PyMC + ArviZ; profile-likelihood and FIM condition-number reporting as a standard artefact of every fit.
    - **inputs**: Multi-rate TGA (blank-corrected), dilatometry matrix, gravity-sag deflections, furnace logs.
    - **outputs**: Posterior over ~20 parameters; an identifiability report naming which parameters the current data CANNOT resolve and which experiment would resolve each; the posterior samples that feed the robust optimiser.
    - **effort**: ~1200 lines, 3 weeks, continuous thereafter.

Not a parameter sweep — a genuine optimal-control solve, because the constraints that bind are constraints on the ORDER of events (get the carbon and oxygen out BEFORE the pores close), which a staircase of hand-tuned holds cannot express and an optimiser can.

- **how_unknown_binder_is_handled**: Six mechanisms, in the order they execute.

(1) ARCHITECTURAL: the binder never appears anywhere in the code as a chemistry. It appears as a measured four-phase inventory — {S_solv: non-network extractable volatile; alpha_1..alpha_N: network pseudo-components; C_char: carbon residue; ash: inorganic residue} — plus a heteroatom vector {P, Ge, Sn, S, Si, Na, Cl} in ppm-of-slurry. Every downstream model consumes only those. Identifying the monomers is interesting for a paper and irrelevant to the cycle; identifying w_binder, the char yield and the exotherm is what the cycle depends on. This decoupling is what lets the project start before the chemistry is known.

(2) THE MASS-BALANCE CLOSER — the single highest-value experiment in the project, 1 day, ~200 EUR. LOI-THEN-REDUCE. Take a cured, solvent-washed, vacuum-dried green coupon, m0 = 2-5 g. Muffle at 600 C in air for 4 h -> m1 (= Cu_xO_y + ash). Transfer to a tube furnace, 4%H2/Ar, 400 C, 2 h -> m2 (= Cu + ash). Then m_binder = m0 - m2 EXACTLY, and the copper's oxygen pickup cancels identically. This is the answer to the trap the brief flags repeatedly: a naive air TGA of a Cu-filled sample is uninterpretable because the copper GAINS +8.0 wt% at 400 C, +14.4 at 600 C and +16.8 at 800 C while the binder is losing only 7.3-13.6 wt%; the oxidation gain is comparable to or larger than the signal. With a 0.1 mg balance on 2 g the closure resolves binder content to +/-0.005 wt% against a 7-14 wt% quantity — roughly 100x better than any TGA inference, and it works without knowing anything at all about the binder. phi_green follows as (m2/8960)/V_geom, giving 0.45-0.60 to +/-0.5 vol%. Triangulated against He pycnometry (AccuPyc II) + wax-sealed Archimedes, and against the switched TGA below; the three must agree to 0.5 wt% or the State Card is rejected by pydantic validation.

(3) THE KINETIC IDENTIFICATION, with the atmosphere trap fixed. The corrected TGA protocol is: run in Ar to 600 C to get the network-scission profile, then SWITCH TO AIR at 600 C within the same run to combust the char and close the mass balance — because an inert-atmosphere TGA systematically under-reports binder content by the ~7 wt% char that an acrylate leaves in argon but not in air. Run a PARALLEL blank of the recovered copper powder (same d50, same crucible, same ramp, same atmosphere sequence) and subtract it. The ingestion layer, src/amculcm/feedstock/ingest_tga.py, REFUSES at runtime to compute alpha from a raw air-TGA of a filled sample with no matching blank — this single guard protects the entire downstream kinetic analysis. Conversion is defined on the blank-corrected mass, with evolved-gas signals (CO2, total hydrocarbon from QMS or FTIR) available as an alternative conversion variable for oxidising runs. Then the ICTAC-2020 stack (Friedman -> Vyazovkin advanced integral -> KAS/OFW auto-disabled when Ea(alpha) varies by more than 15%), model selection over N = 2..4 parallel truncated Sestak-Berggren reactions by BIC, seeded at the random-scission values m = 0.39-0.40, n = 1.02-1.12 and unit-tested against the numerically integrated Simha-Wall solution rather than against a published table whose column headers are inconsistent between sources. Priors from the published M-DAEM fits for LCM-type acrylate networks: E0 = 62.1/215.3/233.1, 102.5/128.1/238.3, 101.1/216.3/199.4 kJ/mol, log10 A = 8/17/19.

(4) THE BINDER-AGNOSTIC SAFE ENVELOPE — what you run in week 1, before any of the above has finished. Take the UNION of worst cases over the plausible binder family {Lithoz LCM acrylate + non-reactive solvent; Incus-type wax thermoplastic; plain HDDA/oligomer}: earliest onset 70 C (solvent, Resch's measured 70-140 C event), latest completion 500 C (air), highest char yield 7 wt% of the organic fraction (argon), highest exotherm 30 MJ/kg, highest binder loading 13.6 wt%, lowest green conductivity 0.80 W/mK. Run the L1 screener under each corner and take the POINTWISE-SLOWEST T(t) and the pointwise-lowest pO2(t). The result is Cycle Card v0, which is conservative by construction and defensible in a paper as "worst-case-envelope cycle synthesis under binder uncertainty" — a method contribution in its own right. The envelope narrows automatically as the State Card fills in, and the code reports the wall-clock hours saved by each measurement, which is how you justify the next experiment to a budget holder.

(5) THE IRREDUCIBLE UNKNOWNS, stated and priced. (a) Photoinitiator heteroatom: at 460 nm only BAPO-class acylphosphine oxides and acylgermanes are practical, so the phosphorus risk is structural, not incidental. ICP-OES/MS on digested green vs recovered powder settles it in a day — and you must assay for P AND Ge AND Sn, not P alone. If BAPO at 0.5-2 wt% of the organic fraction, the all-retained P budget is 30-182 ppm, costing 2.2-13 %IACS at the SKI coefficient of -0.73 %IACS per 10 ppm, i.e. a ceiling of ~350-390 W/mK no matter how perfect the cycle. There is also a nasty coupling: driving oxygen to zero makes the phosphorus penalty WORSE, because phosphates otherwise getter P out of solid solution. (b) The light-absorbing dye, which Lithoz confirms is present in LCM slurries and which is typically aromatic and therefore a prime char precursor — no published copper VPP work used a dye, so their carbon results may be optimistic for this material. Py-GC-MS at 620 C will show it. (c) The physical form of the residual carbon on copper (amorphous vs graphitic vs adsorbed) — unmeasured anywhere in the literature; Raman D/G ratio on interrupted coupons. Each carries an explicit sensitivity line in the auto-generated report.

- **optimal_cycle_formulation**: MULTI-PHASE HYBRID OPTIMAL CONTROL, direct transcription, solved on L1 and verified on L2.

PHASES. Four, with free durations and free switching times t_1 < t_2 < t_3 < t_4: P1 Dry (25-250 C, solvent removal, evaporation-limited), P2 Pyrolyse (200-500 C, exotherm-limited), P3 Carbon+Reduce (450-900 C, gasification- and steam-efflux-limited), P4 Densify (900-1075 C, SOVS). P2 and P3 have completely different rate-limiting physics and are separate solver regimes with separate objective terms. An OUTER enumeration runs the whole solve for each topology in {OX, WGS, HYBRID} — this is essential, because every published copper success used an atmosphere SWITCH and a purely continuous T(t) optimiser will never discover that structure.

DECISION VARIABLES (actuator-space, so the answer is executable). u1 = T_furnace(t) in [20, 1075] C with |dT/dt| <= 10 K/min and a second-derivative slew bound. u2 = x_O2,in in [0, 0.21]. u3 = x_H2,in in [0, 0.04] (hard cap at 4 vol% for the Lin & Hwang steam-embrittlement limit and for lab safety below the 4.1% LFL). u4 = T_dewpoint in [-60, +60] C, mapped to pH2O by Arden Buck p_sat = 6.1121 exp((18.678 - Td/234.5) Td/(257.14+Td)) hPa. u5 = total pressure in [50, 1000] mbar (Roumanie ran H2 at 50-600 mbar). u6 = total flow in [0.5, 10] slpm. Crucially pO2, pH2 and pH2O at the PART SURFACE are algebraic states computed from these by the L3 retort model plus the H2/H2O and water-gas-shift equilibria, not free variables — which is what stops the optimiser from proposing an atmosphere the rig cannot make.

STATES. x = [T_part, S_solv, alpha_1..alpha_3, X_ox, X_red, C_char, rho_rel, G, eps_open, n_O, n_C], 13-15 components.

OBJECTIVE. Primary form: minimise total cycle time t_f, with quality as hard terminal constraints, plus a small control-regularisation w3 = 1e-4 * integral ||u_dot||^2_R dt that keeps the trajectory furnace-executable. Secondary form for the robustness study: J = t_f + w1 * integral (C_char/C_spec)^2 dt + w2 ||rho_f - rho_target||^2. Lombardo & Retzloff's published result — that a CONTINUOUSLY VARYING heating rate beats the staircase of isothermal holds that Lithoz actually ships — is the claim this formulation is built to test, and the 156 h LithaCon-style schedule is provably not optimal, which is a clean quantifiable paper result.

TERMINAL CONSTRAINTS. rho(t_f) >= 0.95; C(t_f) <= 200 ppm (the traceable number: US5302562A shows ~1550 ppm C gives 59% TD from a 58-59% green compact, i.e. densification is entirely inhibited); O(t_f) <= 300 ppm; distortion surrogate <= tolerance.

PATH CONSTRAINTS, ranked by how often they are actually active (I computed these, and the ranking is the opinionated core of the design):
C1 THERMAL, ACTIVE THROUGHOUT P2 AND THE BINDING CONSTRAINT: T_part - T_furnace <= 20 K, and Frank-Kamenetskii delta = Q rho A E L^2/(k_eff R T^2) exp(-E/RT) < 0.88 for a slab. Computed consequence: with 1.25e10 J/m3 of binder combustion and h = 20 W/m2K, holding dT < 20 K requires spreading the burn over 8.7 h at 1 mm half-thickness, 21.7 h at 2.5 mm, 43.4 h at 5 mm and 86.8 h at 10 mm. This derives IFAM's 63 h / 250 C convection-cabinet hold from first principles. The optimiser's response is to schedule pO2 down, not dT/dt down — cutting x_O2 from 0.21 to 0.002 cuts q_gen proportionally in the oxygen-starved limit and buys back an order of magnitude of cycle time. That is the degree of freedom Roumanie screened but never optimised.
C2 ORDERING (carbon): C_char(t) <= 200 ppm whenever rho(t) >= 0.88, written smoothly as integral max(0, C - 2e-4) * max(0, rho - 0.88) dt = 0. The deeper version tracks specific surface area rather than density, because gasification is area-proportional and SSA collapses long before open porosity closes at ~92%.
C3 ORDERING (oxygen): n_O(t) <= 0.2 wt% whenever x_H2 > 100 ppm (Lin & Hwang's explicit design limit), and reduction must complete before rho >= 0.88. These two ordering constraints are what make this an optimal-control problem rather than a curve fit, and they are the mechanism behind both the "incomplete binder removal" explanation and hydrogen blistering.
C4 EUTECTIC: T <= 1066 C whenever local [O] > 0.39 wt%, relaxing to 1075 C only once the posterior mean + 2 sigma of [O] falls below it. Liquid forms 18.6 K BELOW the pure-Cu melting point in any copper above 0.39 wt% O; Roumanie's 1050 C ceiling is not arbitrary, it is 16 K under this eutectic.
C5 STEAM EFFLUX: the local H2O generation rate from oxide reduction must not exceed the Darcy escape rate, i.e. dP_steam < sigma_t(T, alpha). This is the constraint that keeps you off the 673-723 K swelling onset, where coarse Cu powder at 0.471 mass% O swelled ~20% in H2.
C6 ATMOSPHERE THERMODYNAMICS: pH2O/pH2 below the Cu/Cu2O boundary with safety factor SF = 5. I evaluated the boundary from dG(4Cu+O2=2Cu2O) = -333400 + 141.3T and dG(2H2+O2=2H2O) = -492900 + 109.6T: 1.25e8 (300 C), 1.04e7 (400), 1.65e6 (500), 3.97e5 (600), 1.29e5 (700), 5.82e4 (785), 5.13e4 (800), 2.39e4 (900), 1.26e4 (1000), 8.69e3 (1066 C) — reproducing IBM's 785 C production setpoint to within a factor of 5.8 on the reducing side, which is the validation test in tests/test_ellingham_vs_ibm.py. THE OPINIONATED FINDING: this constraint is almost never active. At 1000 C with 1000 ppm H2 you would need a dew point above +50 C to approach the boundary even with 100x margin, and a practical +20 to +40 C dew point is safe everywhere below 1000 C. So atmosphere optimisation during the carbon hold is NOT about protecting the copper — it is a pure trade between gasification rate (proportional to pH2O, product-inhibited by pH2) and the SSA-collapse clock. That reframing is worth a paper section on its own.
C7 PRESSURE, KEPT AS A DIAGNOSTIC AND EXPECTED INACTIVE: dP = mu n_dot R T L^2/(2 K M P) with K = eps^3 d^2/(180(1-eps)^2). Computed for 16 um powder, 5 mm half-thickness, 10% binder per hour at 400 C: 2.4 bar at 2% open porosity, 0.14 bar at 5%, 0.016 bar at 10%, negligible above — against 10-23 MPa (100-230 bar) measured green strength. A 40-100x margin. DEMONSTRATING that this constraint is inactive is itself a publishable figure and it justifies deleting an entire expensive PDE branch. BUT it is a gated deletion: at d50 = 4 um the same calculation gives 38 bar at 2% porosity, which is green strength, so if L0 measures d50 < 6 um the pressure branch is re-enabled automatically by docs/escalation_policy.md.
C8 Furnace slew and MFC/bubbler rate limits from L3.

TRANSCRIPTION AND SOLVER. Radau IIA collocation, degree 3, 60 finite elements per phase, phase durations as free variables. CasADi 3.6 symbolic graph with exact first and second derivatives; IPOPT with MA57 (HSL) or MUMPS. Typical solve 2-15 min on a laptop for 4 phases x 60 elements x 14 states. Warm-started from the literature baseline discretised onto the same grid — the IFAM recipe (acetone solvent debind 24 h; air 24 h/120 C + 63 h/250 C; sinter 2 h/1050 C in 99.999% H2) is a free, industrially validated feasible point and should be used as such. Twenty-start multistart with perturbed initial guesses; the topology enumeration on the outside.

UNCERTAINTY PROPAGATION TO THE CYCLE. Each path constraint g(x,u,theta) <= 0 is replaced by its robust counterpart. Stage 1 (pragmatic, default): worst case over the 8 highest-Sobol parameters on a Sobol-reduced active subspace, which costs 8x. Stage 2 (for the paper): scenario approach over M = 64 posterior samples from L5, giving P(spec met) >= 0.95. The Cycle Card reports, for every terminal spec, the predicted value, the 95% band, and WHICH parameter dominates the band — so the next experiment chooses itself.

OUTPUT. A CycleCard YAML compiled straight to a Eurotherm nanodac or Nabertherm B500 segment program, with the active-constraint timeline attached so the operator can see which physical limit the cycle is riding at every moment.

- **biggest_risks**:
  - THE WGS TOPOLOGY SIMPLY FAILS. The char from a crosslinked acrylate on copper may be partly graphitised and copper is a poor C-H activation catalyst, so steam gasification at 750-850 C may be orders of magnitude too slow — there are NO measured rate constants for this system anywhere, and the only prior is a generic random-pore-model Ea of 176-222 kJ/mol whose transfer error could be 10x. Mitigation: E6 measures the rate directly in week 4-6, and E3 runs both arms simultaneously on identical coupons so the fork is resolved by week 3 for ~2 weeks of furnace time. Arm OX is literature-proven (Roumanie: air debind at 400 C gives 0.019 wt% C, matching the 0.018 wt% raw-powder baseline, across four different acrylate formulations) so the downside is bounded — you lose the elegant route, not the project.
  - THE OXIDATIVE ROUTE CRACKS EVERYTHING. An 8 wt% oxygen pickup means ~64% conversion to Cu2O, which at a Pilling-Bedworth ratio of 1.68 is a +43 vol% / +12.7% linear expansion of the metal phase. Resch measured +3.0% diametral and +7.8% build-axis on real DLP discs under a 600 C air cycle — a 2.6x anisotropy that no ceramic debinding model contains. Mitigation: schedule pO2 down rather than temperature (2000 ppm then 2 vol%, never air), cap the peak debind temperature at 400 C as both CEA and IFAM independently did, use the thickness ladder in E2 to find the maximum safe section, and put the oxidation eigenstrain in the state vector from day one rather than trying to absorb it into a discrepancy term.
  - HYDROGEN DISEASE. If you air-debind you sit at roughly 2 wt% oxygen against Lin & Hwang's explicit limit of 0.2 mass%, i.e. 10x over, with swelling onset at 673-723 K and peak expansion near 1133 K; coarse powder at 0.471 mass% O swelled ~20% in H2. The reduction of an 8 wt% pickup releases 22,400 mol H2O per m3 of green body — 3.5x the entire binder's gas output and roughly 2000 part-volumes of steam at 800 C — all of which must leave before open porosity closes. Mitigation: a mandatory low-pH2 (<=1%) reduction dwell at 300-350 C, BELOW the swelling onset and while the pore network is wide open, run to completion and verified gravimetrically; constraint C3 makes this an ordering constraint the optimiser cannot violate; E10 provokes and calibrates the failure deliberately.
  - PHOSPHORUS CAPS THE CONDUCTIVITY NO MATTER WHAT YOU DO. At 460 nm only BAPO-class acylphosphine oxides and acylgermanes are practical initiators, and the 'less than 1 wt% photoinitiator' figure quoted for translucent ceramics is implausible for a black, strongly absorbing copper slurry — the closest published copper DLP work needed 5 wt% PI on the organic fraction. All-retained P at 0.5-2 wt% PI gives 30-182 ppm, costing 2.2-13 %IACS and capping k at ~350-390 W/mK. There is a perverse coupling: driving oxygen to zero makes the penalty WORSE, because phosphates otherwise getter P out of solid solution. Mitigation: ICP in week 0; if P > 50 ppm, reframe the headline claim around density, defect freedom and cycle time rather than conductivity, and tell Lithoz — a phosphorus-free initiator is their problem to solve, not yours.
  - IDENTIFIABILITY THEATRE. The sigma_s0/eta0 degeneracy in free sintering is EXACT (FIM condition number 1.5e12, null direction (-0.708, -0.706, ~0)), and it is easy to run an expensive sinter-forging campaign that does not fix it — because the trace of the SOVS rate is phi-free even under an applied uniaxial load, so measuring only density or only axial length under load is exactly as blind as free dilatometry. Mitigation: the gravity-sag overhang bar with dual-axis strain; lump A0/alpha as Cabo Rios does and never claim a measured surface energy from a fitted sigma_s0; hold Olevsky's seven theoretical closure constants fixed so the real fit is 2-4 parameters, not 11; and report FIM condition numbers as a standard artefact of every calibration.
  - THE POWDER IS FINE. Every published vat-photopolymerisation copper feedstock is COARSE (9/16/25 um, 11/20/40 um, d50 22 um) because coarse particles scatter less and cure deeper — but if Lithoz has used H2-reduced fine powder to hit a 40 um pixel, d50 could be 4-5 um. Permeability then drops 16x and the computed Darcy over-pressure at 2% open porosity goes from 2.4 bar to 38 bar, which is green strength — re-activating an entire PDE branch the architecture deliberately demoted. Mitigation: this is a gate, not a surprise. E0 measures d50 in an hour and docs/escalation_policy.md re-enables the branch automatically below 6 um.
  - SCOPE CREEP INTO THE 3-D MODEL. The single most likely way this project fails is that six months go into a beautiful MOOSE model of a fictional material while no parts come out of the furnace. Mitigation: the escalation policy is written as numeric triggers in the repo, L4 is explicitly gated behind Phase 3, and Phase 1's gate is 'real parts, week 4'. If the group finds itself calibrating an FE model before it has a measured State Card, the architecture has been abandoned.
  - MODEL-FORM ERROR LAUNDERED THROUGH A DISCREPANCY TERM. A Kennedy-O'Hagan delta(x) will happily absorb a missing 22,400 mol/m3 source term and then extrapolate catastrophically to a different thickness. Mitigation: tests/test_no_discrepancy_before_physics.py refuses to enable the term until solvent evaporation, copper oxidation and reduction steam are all in the state vector and the residual passes a whiteness test; and Roumanie's published thin-gear (0.022 wt% O) vs thick-disc (0.062 wt% O) pair under an identical cycle is used as an out-of-sample extrapolation test that the model must pass before any cycle is trusted at a new thickness.
  - THE Cu-Cu2O EUTECTIC AT 1066 C. Liquid forms 18.6 K below the pure-copper melting point in any copper above 0.39 wt% oxygen; native oxide alone can reach ~0.575 wt% O on a powder. Roumanie's 1050 C ceiling is 16 K under it, not an arbitrary choice. Mitigation: hard state-dependent constraint C4 with the ceiling relaxing toward 1075 C only when the posterior mean plus two standard deviations of [O] falls below 0.39 wt%; track [O] as a field in L2/L4, not a scalar.
  - THE TOOLING IS NOT WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE. PUMA is Argonne code, not ORNL, has zero CI (no .github directory exists), pins its submodules to a personal fork's moving branch, is 1514 lines of thermoelastic-only mechanics, and contains NO sintering code whatsoever — grep -ril sinter over the whole repository matches only README.md. Building on it would stake a multi-year project on an unreproducible dependency. Mitigation: build on upstream idaholab/moose with its vendored, CI-tested NEML2, and lift PUMA's six coupled kernels as patterns. Separately, verify NEML2's sintering stress form (3*gamma*phi^2/r, which vanishes at full density) against the classical Olevsky form (3*gamma*rho^2/r, which peaks there) BEFORE any calibration — they differ qualitatively in exactly the late-stage regime that decides whether copper reaches target density, and the fix is ~60 lines of PyTorch.
  - MUCH OF THE ANCHOR LITERATURE IS UNVERIFIED. The research sweep flags that the entire Ott dissertation parameter set (densification onset 800 C, peak rate 0.63 %/min at 895 C, the rho(t) fit whose units are printed as 1/s but must be 1/h, the oxide growth law d_ox = 3.66 t^0.266), and essentially all of the Metals 2024 14:1268 debinding content including the acetone/95.3% density claim, could not be opened. Mitigation: data/literature/anchors.yaml carries a per-number `verified: true|false` flag, unverified numbers are auto-tagged ANALOGUE with inflated sigma, and someone with institutional access opens both PDFs in week 1 — a one-hour task that de-risks several downstream parameters.
  - THE FURNACE CANNOT MAKE THE ATMOSPHERE THE OPTIMISER ASKS FOR. A humidified low-H2 gas at a controlled dew point, with ppm-level H2 and a zirconia oxygen sensor on the exhaust, is not standard university kit. Mitigation: check this in week 0, before Phase 0's gate. A bubbler with a temperature-controlled bath, a heated transfer line, a Michell dew-point meter, a premixed 4%H2/N2 cylinder and three Bronkhorst MFCs is roughly 8 kEUR and is by far the highest-leverage purchase in the project — without it, atmosphere is a label rather than a control variable and the whole architecture degenerates to a T(t) optimiser, which the literature shows finds a bad local optimum by construction.
- **why_this_beats_alternatives**: The obvious alternative — build the coupled 3-D thermo-chemo-mechanical FE model first, calibrate it, then derive the cycle — fails for three reasons that are specific to THIS material rather than general.

First, it would be a high-fidelity model of a fictional material. Every material parameter that matters is unknown: the binder fraction, the char yield, the powder d50, the green conductivity (currently bounded across two orders of magnitude, 0.80 to 160 W/mK), the green strength as a function of temperature and conversion, and the copper sintering constitutive parameters, for which no calibrated SOVS/Abouaf/Riedel parameter set exists anywhere in the literature. The dominant uncertainty is not modelling error — it is a one-day bench measurement. Spending three months on mesh convergence while w_binder is unknown to within a factor of two is a category error, and the LOI-then-reduce closure retires more uncertainty per euro than any amount of FE work.

Second, I checked which constraints are actually active, and the expensive physics is not among them. The Darcy over-pressure for a 16 um copper powder at 2% open porosity and 5 mm half-thickness is 2.4 bar against a measured green strength of 10-23 MPa — a 40-100x margin, falling to 0.016 bar by 10% porosity. The Biot number is 2e-4 to 0.67 across every realistic case, so a conjugate heat-transfer solve inside the part is wasted computation. The Cu/Cu2O reducing margin is two to three orders of magnitude wide at any practical dew point below 1000 C. Meanwhile the constraint that genuinely binds is a lumped 0-D energy balance — the exotherm — and its scaling is purely geometric (dT_surf = q*L/h), which is why a 5 mm half-thickness needs the binder burn spread over 43 h and why IFAM's 63 h / 250 C hold exists. A 0-D model captures the binding constraint exactly; a 3-D model captures it identically but 10^5 times slower, which matters enormously when the optimiser needs 10^5 evaluations.

Third, the papers come out of the measurement campaign, not out of the model. The literature sweep identified five clean, cheap, confirmed-empty gaps: no master sintering curve exists for pure copper; no sigma_t(T, alpha) exists for any metal-filled photopolymer; no core-versus-surface temperature measurement exists for any metal green body during pyrolysis; no char gasification rate constants exist on a copper surface; and no permeability measurement, isotropic or anisotropic, exists for any vat-photopolymerised body. Every one of those is a dilatometer run, a hot bend test, a thermocouple, an isothermal TGA and a pressure-drop rig — weeks of work, not months — and none of them requires the 3-D model to exist first. The genuinely novel simulation contribution, the coupled oxygen-in / oxygen-out loop for copper VPP, is a 0-D/1-D result with a 3-D confirmation, not a 3-D result.

Against the opposite extreme — pure design of experiments, run a Taguchi array and pick the best cycle — this architecture wins because the binding constraints are constraints on the ORDER of events (all carbon and oxygen must leave before pore closure; reduction must complete before densification; temperature must stay below 1066 C while oxygen remains). Orderings are exactly what optimal control expresses and exactly what a factorial screen cannot. It also wins because the design space is (T, pO2, pH2, dew point, total pressure, and switching times) — at least six dimensions with a combinatorial topology choice on top, where Roumanie's own screen showed a 20x swing in residual carbon from atmosphere alone while temperature, dwell and pressure did essentially nothing. A DoE that varies temperature will find nothing; a DoE that varies all six is unaffordable.

And against the pure-optimal-control-purist alternative — write the full multi-phase OCP over the true PDE from the start — it wins on the schedule that actually matters. The group needs a defensible cycle in the furnace in weeks. Cycle Card v0 is generated in week 1 from a worst-case envelope over the plausible binder family, which is conservative by construction and publishable as a method. Cycle Card v1 arrives in week 4 with real parts behind it. Everything after that is earned: the escalation policy in docs/escalation_policy.md is written as numeric triggers (d50 below 6 um re-enables the pressure branch; a measured core-surface delta above 30 K escalates L1 to L2; shrinkage anisotropy above 1.5 percentage points escalates to L4), so sophistication is added because evidence demanded it and the demand is auditable. The digital twin grows out of the measurement campaign instead of preceding it, which is also, not coincidentally, the only way it ends up calibrated.
