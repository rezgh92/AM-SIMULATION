Simulating a copper slurry whose binder nobody will tell us

An architecture for predicting debinding, sintering, shrinkage and distortion of the Lithoz R&D copper photopolymer on the CeraFab Multi 2M30 — and for deriving an optimum thermal cycle without knowing the binder chemistry or its content.

CeraFab Multi 2M30 · 35 µm · 450 nm Binder: proprietary, undisclosed Cu: no carbide · eutectic 1066 °C Evidence: 27 agent reports
01

Three findings that reshape the project

These came out of a ten-dimension literature and data sweep, three competing architectures, and three adversarial design reviews that independently recomputed every load-bearing number.

Regime

You mostly do not need to know the binder

Oxidative debinding of a metal-filled photopolymer is not kinetics-limited. It is limited by oxygen delivery and heat removal — so the binder's pre-exponential factor has essentially no leverage on the part-scale cycle.

δ = 9.5 → 1.7×10⁵ vs δ_c = 0.88
Structure

It is an ordering problem, not a temperature problem

Copper forms no carbide, so carbon leaves only by gasification. Once porosity closes, residual carbon and reduction steam are sealed in permanently. Both clocks must finish first.

pore closure ≈ 92 % rel. density
Missing physics

A reaction every independent design missed

Cu₂O + C → 2Cu + CO is spontaneous above 76 °C — carbon and copper oxide cannot coexist. It is simultaneously the real carbon sink and, because CO is insoluble in copper, the real blistering source.

ΔG = 55 200 − 158.3·T J/mol

The design law that makes this tractable

In the heat-removal-limited regime the minimum debinding time has a closed form with no fitted parameters. Temperature does not appear in it.

tmin  =  ΔHc · ρb · (V/A)  /  (h · ΔTcrit) 47.7 h at 5 mm half-thickness, h = 20 W/m²K, ΔT_crit = 20 K  ·  9.5 h at h = 100  ·  reproduces two published industrial cycles with zero fitted parameters

Three consequences a human schedule-writer will not reach: the optimal debind is near-isothermal with pO₂ as the throttle, not a temperature staircase; time scales linearly with half-thickness, not quadratically; and the real lever is h, the gas-side heat transfer coefficient — a baffle and higher flow, order 1 k€, compresses a 5 mm part from 47.7 h to 9.5 h at identical thermal risk.

The measurement that decides it

All of the above holds at k_eff = 1 W/m·K. At 10 W/m·K — plausible if the copper particles percolate — δ falls to 0.95 at 1 mm, i.e. marginal rather than supercritical, and the claim degrades to "binder kinetics are irrelevant above roughly 2 mm section."

A laser-flash k_eff on a cured coupon is therefore the single most consequential week-1 measurement in the project.

Corrections that must be applied

  • The eutectic ceiling is against the solidus, not the eutectic composition. Oxygen solubility in Cu at 1066 °C is ~0.008 wt% (≈80 ppm), not 0.39 wt%. Any part above ~100 ppm O begins to melt at 1066 °C — so that is a near-permanent ceiling until deoxidation is complete, removing the window where copper densifies fastest.
  • Pore-gas back-pressure is the terminal-density limiter, and nobody had it. σs,eff = 2γ/r − pgas. Just 30 bar of trapped gas at r = 1 µm halts densification entirely. Closed-pore H₂O/CO/CO₂ are all insoluble in copper — unlike H₂, which is exactly why hydrogen sintering works.
  • Hydrogen embrittlement is not a Darcy problem. It is lattice H diffusing to internal Cu₂O, making insoluble H₂O at grain boundaries. A Darcy efflux constraint will certify parts that embrittle. The control is the ordering constraint.
  • Frank-Kamenetskii δ < 0.88 must never be a hard path constraint. Two of the three designs imposed it; solving δ(T) = 0.88 caps the debind at 158–214 °C, where nothing decomposes, making their own optimiser infeasible. Keep δ as a regime diagnostic only.
  • Phosphorus. At 450 nm only BAPO-class acylphosphine oxides and acylgermanes are practical initiators, and the Cu–Cu₃P eutectic is at 714 °C — trace P gives liquid phase mid-debind. Assay for P, Ge, Sn and S, not P alone.
02

Architecture

Five tiers plus one instrument. Each tier is independently testable, and each has a written numeric trigger for when the tier above it gets built — model complexity is pre-registered, not decided after the fact.

T0

Thermochemical feasibility

No fitted parameter ever lives here

Cu–C–O–H predominance diagram; Ellingham lines including the carbothermic channel; Arden Buck dew-point ↔ pH₂O actuator map; water-gas shift; solidus-based eutectic ceiling. Verified in CI against IBM's 785 °C production setpoint.

T1

The digital coupon

0-D / 1-D reduced-order model

13–15 state DAE: thermal, N-component Šesták-Berggren decomposition, parabolic oxidation with PBR eigenstrain, JMAK-with-incubation reduction, carbothermic Cu₂O + C, gasification, Darcy + Klinkenberg with a pre-percolation branch, SOVS with pore-gas back-pressure. NumPy reference and CasADi graph, CI-tested to agree to 1e-8.

T2

Retort model

Makes the answer executable

CSTR tanks-in-series oxygen balance, measured h, radiative lag, MFC and bubbler slew limits. Surface pO₂, pH₂ and pH₂O become algebraic states — so the optimiser physically cannot request an atmosphere the rig can't make.

T3

Identification & triage

Runs before furnace hours are spent

Morris → Sobol → profile likelihood, producing an IDENTIFIED / MARGINALISED / IRRELEVANT verdict per parameter. Isokinetic reparameterisation kills a −0.9999 lnA–E ridge in one line. Hierarchical lot effects. Kennedy-O'Hagan discrepancy on constraint margins only, gated behind a whiteness test.

T4

Chance-constrained optimal control

The deliverable layer

CasADi + IPOPT, Radau IIA collocation, free switching times, outer enumeration over atmosphere topologies {OX, WGS, HYBRID}. Delivers a Pareto front, not a cycle — and compiles to a furnace segment program with the active-constraint timeline attached.

T5

3-D thermo-chemo-mechanical

Escalation only

MOOSE + NEML2. Anisotropic sintering stress, gravity, setter friction, inverse shrinkage compensation. Built only when shrinkage anisotropy exceeds 1.5 percentage points.

OBS

Exhaust-gas observer

Zirconia pO₂ + dew point + NDIR CO₂

All three adversarial reviewers independently named this the best single idea in the set. It measures, in real time and without knowing anything about the binder, exactly the two quantities every ordering constraint is written on. It converts binder ignorance from a marginalisation problem into a feedback problem, and it is the only instrument that would detect the carbothermic CO burst. Buy it in week 1.

03

The unknown-binder strategy

The principle: do not try to discover the chemistry — make it mostly irrelevant, and quantify the residue.

The trap that invalidates the obvious method

Copper gains mass by oxidation while the binder loses it: +8.0 wt% at 400 °C, +14.4 at 600 °C, +16.8 at 800 °C — against a binder inventory of only 7.3–13.6 wt%. A raw air-TGA of a filled sample can return a negative binder content. Standard burn-off does not work here.

  1. Decouple architecturallyThe binder never appears in the code as a chemistry — only as a measured inventory {solvent, network pseudo-components, char, ash} plus a heteroatom vector in ppm. Identifying monomers is interesting for a paper and irrelevant to the cycle.
  2. Close the mass balance: LOI-then-reduceOne day, ~200 €, the highest-value experiment in the project. Coupon → 600 °C air → tube furnace 4 %H₂/Ar → m_binder = m₀ − m₂, and the oxygen pickup cancels identically. With the guards reviewers demanded: crush the cake, reduce to constant mass, run a bare-powder control, LECO C and O on the residue. Honest accuracy ±0.2–0.5 wt%, not the ±0.005 that is balance resolution.
  3. Triangulate three independent routesLOI-then-reduce, He pycnometry + wax-sealed Archimedes, and switched TGA. They must agree to 0.5 wt% or the feedstock card is rejected by schema validation.
  4. The corrected TGA protocolRamp in argon to 600 °C, then switch to air isothermally in the same run to combust the char and close the balance. Matched bare-Cu blank, subtracted — and an ingestion layer that refuses at runtime to compute conversion without one. Four heating rates, ICTAC-2020 isoconversional stack, blind prediction of a fifth. Always on printed cured material, never neat resin: copper catalyses the decomposition.
  5. Ship a worst-case envelope in week 1Union of worst cases over the plausible binder family, pointwise-slowest T(t) and pointwise-lowest pO₂(t). Conservative by construction, it narrows automatically as measurements land — and the code reports the wall-clock hours saved by each measurement, which is how the next experiment gets funded.
  6. Name and price the irreducible unknownsThe photoinitiator heteroatom; the light-absorbing dye (no published copper VPP study used one, so their encouraging carbon results may be systematically optimistic for this material — the largest threat to external validity); and the physical form of residual carbon, where speciation matters more than ppm.
  7. Ask Lithoz — don't let pride add three weeksUnder NDA: organic volume fraction; powder grade, D10/50/90, morphology, and as-received oxygen in ppm; initiator class as a yes/no; dye present; and any internal thermal envelope even flagged unvalidated. Also: confirm single-batch supply in writing, keep a sealed reserve, and settle publication terms before the first experiment.
04

The baseline cycle

Binder-agnostic worst-case envelope, corrections applied. It exists so the furnace is not idle while the model is built — and so the first validation gate has in-house anchor data with known loading, gas velocity and thermocouple placement.

Design intent: throttle oxygen hard throughout rather than debinding in air. That is the lever the regime analysis identifies anyway, and it has a second benefit — it prevents the bulk oxidation (up to 63 % conversion to Cu₂O, +43 vol%) that creates the entire steam-reduction and blistering problem downstream. Accept more char, then gasify it in wet H₂ at 700–850 °C where C + H₂O is kinetically viable and porosity is still open.

A number we corrected

The underlying research report quoted ~78 h for this segment list. It is wrong — the segments sum to 146.4 h, with the 0.05 K/min ramps through 205–400 °C accounting for 65 h on their own.

The correction improves the reading. v0 offers essentially no time advantage over Lithoz's 156 h alumina debind — and it is not supposed to. It is a safe envelope whose job is to generate anchor data. The meaningful comparison is against the design law, which puts the exotherm-limited floor for a 5 mm part at 47.7 h. Closing that 1.57× gap, and then attacking the floor itself by raising h, is the optimiser's entire job.

05

Experimental campaign

Ordered by information per euro. The slurry, not the furnace, is the binding resource — size the campaign against what was actually delivered and hold a reserve.

Decouple from the printer

There is no vat parameter set for this slurry, the 450 nm working curve for a copper slurry is unpublished, and 8.96 g/cm³ particles sediment over a long build. Run the feedstock campaign on flood-cured slurry cast in silicone moulds. This removes an unbounded schedule risk from the critical path; printing is derisked in parallel.

Run a reference-material control first. Putting the whole pipeline through a commercial copper MIM feedstock, or an alumina LCM slurry with a published Lithoz cycle, costs perhaps three weeks and substantially derisks both the project and the paper — you demonstrate the capability works before pointing it at a material whose binder you cannot see.

The one gate that changes the project

If the E6 interrupted ladder shows carbon still present at pore closure, the project pivots to a reformulation conversation with Lithoz rather than continuing to optimise a cycle that cannot exist.

What this will and will not predict

Well: shrinkage factors, safe ramp rates, cycle-time scaling with section thickness, the ordering of chemical events, and relative comparisons between candidate cycles. Not reliably: absolute final density to better than ~2 percentage points, and absolute probability of cracking on a specific geometry — those depend on flaw populations and binder details that no amount of inference recovers. The deliverable is therefore a cycle with a credible interval and a chance constraint, never a single trajectory presented as truth.

07

Decisions needed before anything is built

Seven choices that change what gets built. The rest I can take defaults on.

Furnace capability — audit, don't assume

H₂-rated retort with interlocks? Dew-point control hardware — a bubbler and a dew-point meter? 1100 °C at ±5 °C across the load, measured by survey? Tar trap? Without dew-point control, none of the atmosphere optimisation is executable. Is the dilatometer atmosphere-capable — and note push-rod loads of 20–50 cN will creep a brown body and give false shrinkage, so contactless optical is strongly preferred.

Scope: cycle only, or cycle + distortion compensation?

The 3-D tier roughly doubles the project and changes the experiment matrix. If dimensional accuracy on real geometry is a deliverable it must be in from the start. If the goal is dense, crack-free copper, T0–T4 suffices and the roadmap ends around week 12.

How hard do we push Lithoz?

The NDA ask-list costs nothing scientifically and could remove months. Is there an existing agreement, and who owns that conversation? Are publication terms already settled — including a review window and possible co-authorship?

How much slurry is there, and is it one batch?

This is the binding resource, not furnace time. If a second lot with different binder arrives mid-project, every identified kinetic parameter is void — so single-batch confirmation in writing and a sealed retained sample matter now, not later.

Powder D50 — do we know it?

It flips an architectural decision outright. At 16 µm the Darcy pressure branch is deleted; below 6 µm it is re-enabled and the model gets meaningfully more expensive.

Who builds what?

T0–T4 is a strong Python engineer plus a materials scientist. T5 needs someone who can write MOOSE/NEML2 constitutive models — a distinct and scarce skill. Who is hydrogen-trained? Where is LECO run, and what is the turnaround?

Reference-material control — in or out?

Three weeks to run the pipeline on a known system first. I recommend it; it is the cheapest insurance against a referee asking whether the capability was ever demonstrated on something checkable.

Evidence base. 27 verbatim agent reports: ten research dimensions each with an adversarial deep-dive; three competing architectures written to opposed design biases; three hostile design reviews under physical-correctness, feasibility and novelty lenses; one completeness critique. The reviewers independently recomputed load-bearing numbers and caught a 4.8-decade error in one architecture's headline pressure result, a path constraint that made two designs infeasible, and the missing carbothermic reaction shared by all three.
