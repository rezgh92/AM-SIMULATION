Cycle optimiser

Builds the shortest cycle that passes all ten checks for the current inputs and furnace. It moves the furnace in 15-minute steps, each time taking the fastest ramp and gas that keeps every risk index under 80 % of its limit and still leaves the part recoverable.

PHASE A

Debind

N₂ or O₂-throttled N₂. Ramp limited by gas pressure, self-heating and the guard ramp while binder remains.

PHASE B

Clean

Wet forming gas at a hold temperature TB. Steam gasifies char, H₂ reduces oxide. Ends when the core is clean.

PHASE C

Densify

Dry forming gas up to the Cu–O solidus minus your margin. Ends at the density target or when densification stalls.

PHASE D

Cool

Fastest ramp the thermal-stress limit and the furnace allow, reducing gas kept on down to 600 °C.

    What is inside

    Physics in the 1-D coupon

    • Heat conduction with furnace lag, radiation and H₂-dependent convection
    • Three-channel binder pyrolysis plus oxidative degradation, char yield and heat release
    • Pore gas: diffusion, Knudsen flow and pyrolysis blowing; retort gas balance
    • Cu oxidation and H₂ reduction, char gasification by steam, carbothermic reduction
    • Olevsky viscous sintering with Frost–Ashby diffusion, carbon inhibition, grain growth, trapped gas

    Checks it has passed

    • Sintering viscosity fitted to CEA-LITEN DLP copper (fη = 0.28)
    • Out-of-sample Cu compact oxidation (Roumanie): within 30 %
    • Mesh convergence of every risk index; carbon and energy balances within 2 %
    • Closed-form sintering rate to 0.1 %; 3-D free-cube density to 0.3 %
    • This browser engine matches the Python reference on 99.9 % of all terms

    Where it is weakest

    • No TGA of this binder yet: pyrolysis peaks are acrylate analogues
    • Char gasification rate on copper has no published constant
    • Sintering is calibrated on 22 µm powder; recalibrate fη from 3–4 interrupted runs with calipers
    • Cracking is predicted from stress/strength ratios, not fracture mechanics
